OmniScript Data Inspector

This tool lives in DevTools, not here.

  1. Open the page with your OmniScript.
  2. Open DevTools — F12, or ⌥⌘I on a Mac.
  3. Pick the OmniScript tab, next to Elements and Console.

It can only read the page while DevTools is open. To catch remote actions fired during page load, reload with DevTools already open.
